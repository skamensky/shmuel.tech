Shmuel Automation

Shmuel Automation is a personal command-line tool used by Shmuel Kamensky to work with his own Google account.

This tool is for its operator's personal use. It is not offered as a public service and does not invite third-party account registrations.

What it does

The tool lets its operator run requested automations involving Gmail, Contacts, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Tasks, and Apps Script. Depending on the command, it can search and retrieve information, create or update content, download files and attachments, and send or organize information.

Why it requests Google data

Google account data is requested only to carry out commands initiated by the operator. The particular data used depends on the command and the permissions approved during Google's authorization flow.
